People & Culture

Find an answer. Get the right help.

Check the answers below. If you still need help, submit a Support Ticket so People & Culture has the details needed to review your request.

Where do I find my pay stubs?

Sign in to Workstream Worker Hub to view your pay statements.

If you cannot sign in or find the statement you need, submit a Support Ticket. Choose the Worker Hub access issue or Something else, and include the pay date.

I have a pay stub, but no direct deposit. What should I do?

Check the payment method on your stub. If it says Manual Payment, follow the answer below. If it says Direct deposit, check your bank information in Worker Hub and contact your bank or credit union.

If Workstream emails you about a returned payment, follow its instructions. Banking updates can take 3–5 business days.

Still need help? Submit a Support Ticket and choose I have a pay stub, but no direct deposit. Include the pay date and what you have already checked.

My stub says Manual Payment. Will I receive a paper check?

Manual Payment does not request or authorize a paper check. Paper checks require People & Culture approval.

Add or update your direct deposit information in Worker Hub by Monday at 9 AM for the next regular paycheck. Payments follow the regular payroll schedule. People & Culture cannot enter banking information for you.

Need assistance? Submit a Support Ticket and choose My payment method says Manual Payment.

How do I change my direct deposit information?

Add or update your banking information through your own Worker Hub account. Review the information carefully before saving.

If you get stuck, submit a Support Ticket and choose I want to update my direct deposit. If you cannot sign in, choose the Worker Hub access issue instead.

Do not include bank account numbers, routing numbers, passwords, or Social Security numbers in your ticket.

I am missing hours from my paycheck. Where do I start?

Ask your General Manager to review your time records. Provide the dates and clock-in and clock-out times you believe are missing. If time needs correcting, your GM must submit a Payroll Adjustment (PPA) before People & Culture can process the correction.

If you have spoken with your GM and still need help, submit a Support Ticket about missing hours. Include the pay date, shifts in question, and who you spoke with.

Where do I find my W-2 or other tax documents?

Start in Worker Hub. If you cannot find the document, submit a Support Ticket about tax documents and include the tax year you need.

I cannot sign in to Worker Hub. What should I do?

Use the email address from your application. If you only see applications, ask your GM to send your Worker Hub invitation.

If you still cannot sign in, submit a Support Ticket and choose I need help accessing Worker Hub. Tell us what happens when you try and include the error message, if there is one.

I no longer have access to the email or phone on my account. Can you help?

Submit a Support Ticket and choose I need to update the email or phone used to sign in. The form will ask for the information we need to verify your identity and an email address you can access now.

Please do not include your password or Social Security number.

Who handles schedule changes and time-off requests?

Start with your General Manager for schedule changes and time-off approval. Submitting a Support Ticket does not approve time off.

For a vacation balance, leave, or missing time-off pay issue, submit a Support Ticket and choose the matching topic. Include the relevant dates and what needs reviewing.

I have a benefits or 401(k) question. How do I get help?

Submit a Support Ticket and choose the matching topic under Benefits & time off. Tell us which benefit you need help with and whether your question concerns eligibility, enrollment, or existing coverage.

For insurance cards, the form will point you to the appropriate next step based on whether you are enrolled.

How do I report a workplace concern?

Submit a Support Ticket and choose I want to report a workplace concern. You do not need your GM’s permission to report a concern.

Describe what happened, when and where it happened, and who was involved. You may submit without your name or contact information. Providing a way to reach you helps us ask follow-up questions. Reports are handled discreetly.

I need leave or an accommodation. Where do I start?

Submit a Support Ticket and choose I need leave or an accommodation. Include the type of assistance you need and when you need it to begin.

Please leave medical documents and detailed diagnoses out of this initial request.

What happens after I submit a ticket?

You will see a confirmation screen when your submission has been sent to People & Culture. If you provided contact information, we can follow up with you.

Our hours are weekdays, 9 AM–4 PM Eastern. Allow up to one business day for a response. If you have more details about the same issue, include them in your existing conversation when we follow up.

My question is not listed. Can I still submit a ticket?

Yes. Open a Support Ticket and choose Something else. Include your restaurant location, what you need help with, and the best way to reach you.

Need us to take a look?

Use a Support Ticket for pay, account access, benefits, and workplace concerns. The form asks questions based on what you need help with.
