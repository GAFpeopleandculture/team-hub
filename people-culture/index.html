Find an answer. Get the right help.

Check the answers below. If you still need help, submit a Support Ticket so People & Culture has the details needed to review your request.

Where do I find my pay stubs?

Sign in to Workstream Worker Hub to view your pay statements.

If you cannot sign in or find the statement you need, submit a Support Ticket. Choose the Worker Hub access issue or Something else, and include the pay date.

I have a pay stub, but no direct deposit. What should I do?

Check the payment method on your pay stub. For Manual Payment, see the next answer. For Direct deposit, check your information in Worker Hub and contact your bank or credit union.

If a deposit is returned, Payroll must wait for the return to be processed. Updating your banking information does not immediately resend a previously issued payment. Timing depends on the return and verification process. Once your account is updated, allow 3–5 days for that update to process.

Submit a Support Ticket if you still need help. Include the pay date and what you have checked. Do not include banking numbers.

My stub says Manual Payment. Will I receive a paper check?

Manual Payment does not request or authorize a paper check. Paper checks require People & Culture approval.

Update your banking information in Worker Hub by Monday at 9 AM. A change made after payroll processing has begun may not take effect until a later paycheck. People & Culture cannot enter banking information for you.

Submit a Support Ticket and choose My payment method says Manual Payment if you need help.

How do I change my direct deposit information?

Add or update your banking information through your own Worker Hub account. Review the information carefully before saving.

If you get stuck, submit a Support Ticket and choose I want to update my direct deposit. If you cannot sign in, choose the Worker Hub access issue instead.

Do not include bank account numbers, routing numbers, passwords, or Social Security numbers in your ticket.

I am missing hours from my paycheck. Where do I start?

Start with your GM. Provide the dates and hours you believe are missing.

If a correction is needed, your GM must review the records and initiate the Payroll Adjustment process. Submission does not mean the adjustment has completed approval or payroll processing.

If you still need help, submit a Support Ticket for missing hours. Include the pay date, dates/hours affected, and manager contacted.

Where do I find my W-2 or other tax documents?

Start in Worker Hub. If you cannot find your W-2 or other tax document, submit a Support Ticket about tax documents and include the tax year.

If you found your W-2 but believe information on it is incorrect, submit a Support Ticket for a tax-document correction instead of an access issue. Tell us the tax year and which field you believe needs review. Do not enter your Social Security number in the ticket.

I cannot sign in to Worker Hub. What should I do?

Use the email address from your application to sign in to Worker Hub.

If you only see applications and not your employee/Worker Hub information, submit a Worker Hub access ticket. Tell us what you see after signing in. Include an error message if there is one, but never your password.

I no longer have access to the email or phone on my account. Can you help?

Submit a Support Ticket and choose I need to update the email or phone used to sign in. The form will ask for the information we need to verify your identity and an email address you can access now.

Please do not include your password or Social Security number.

Who handles schedule changes and time-off requests?

Your GM handles scheduling and approval of time away from work. Submitting a Support Ticket does not approve time off.

People & Culture can help when the question is about your PTO balance, eligibility, an approved request missing from your pay, or another PTO record/payroll issue.

Submit a Support Ticket with the relevant dates and what needs reviewing.

I have a benefits or 401(k) question. How do I get help?

Submit a Benefits Support Ticket. Choose Medical, Dental, Vision, 401(k), Benefit deduction, Enrollment, Insurance card, or Other in the benefit question.

Tell us whether you need help with eligibility, enrollment, deductions, or existing coverage. For insurance cards, the form will provide guidance based on whether you are enrolled.

How do I report a workplace concern?

Submit a Support Ticket and choose I want to report a workplace concern. You do not need your GM’s permission. Tell us what happened, when and where, and who was involved.

You may submit without your name or contact information. A way to reach you helps us ask follow-up questions.

If you have texts, screenshots, emails, or other records related to your concern, keep them available. You may be asked to provide them.

People & Culture will review your concern and follow up as needed.

I need leave or an accommodation. Where do I start?

Submit a Support Ticket and choose I need leave or an accommodation. Tell us what assistance you need and when it needs to begin. Leave detailed diagnoses and medical documents out of the initial ticket.

If documentation is needed, People & Culture will tell you what to provide and how to submit it. Do not give medical records to the restaurant unless People & Culture specifically directs you to do so.

Contact People & Culture to get started. We can provide an FMLA form for you to return to us and explain what documentation is needed for your request.

What happens after I submit a ticket?

A confirmation screen appears after your submission is sent. If you provided contact information, People & Culture can follow up.

People & Culture is available Monday–Friday, 9 AM–4 PM Eastern. We strive to respond within 12–24 business hours.

My question is not listed. Can I still submit a ticket?

Yes. Open a Support Ticket and choose Something else. Include your restaurant location, what you need help with, and the best way to reach you.

My payroll adjustment was submitted or approved. When will I get paid?

A Payroll Adjustment corrects a verified payroll issue, such as missing hours, an incorrect pay rate, or missed approved PTO. An adjustment is not an immediate or same-day payment.

If your manager says an adjustment has already been submitted, ask whether it has also received the required approval. If you still need help, submit a Support Ticket for a payroll adjustment. Include the pay date, dates/hours being corrected, when it was submitted, and whether you were told it was approved.

Do not rely on a specific payment date until People & Culture confirms it.

Payroll Adjustments generally take 7–14 days from the date the adjustment is approved to process. This is a processing estimate, not a guaranteed payment date.

My vacation, sick pay, or other paid time off was approved, but it wasn’t on my paycheck. What should I do?

First check the pay period and pay statement to make sure you are reviewing the paycheck that corresponds to the time-off dates. Then confirm with your GM when the request was approved.

Approval after payroll has already been finalized may require a Payroll Adjustment rather than an immediate payment.

If the approved time is still missing, submit a Support Ticket for missing time-off pay.

How do I update my tax withholding or tax forms?

Employees can update their own tax withholding information through Workstream Worker Hub. People & Culture cannot complete or change an employee’s tax elections for them.

If you cannot access the tax section or receive an error, submit a Support Ticket for tax withholding help. Do not include completed tax-form data or your Social Security number.

I need proof of employment or wages. How do I request it?

If an apartment complex, lender, government agency, benefits program, or another organization needs employment or wage information, submit a Support Ticket for employment verification.

Tell us what information is being requested. If the request will go directly to another organization, include its contact information and any authorization or instructions you received.

Need to send supporting screenshots or forms? Email them to help@gaf-boj.com. Include your name, restaurant location, and what the attachment relates to so we can match it to your ticket.

People & Culture can complete third-party verification forms or prepare a custom letter. There is no set turnaround time. Include any requested deadline; submission does not guarantee delivery by that date.

I’m no longer employed. How do I request my separation notice?

Submit a Support Ticket and choose the separation/employment-records option. Include the company and restaurant where you worked and your approximate last day worked.

If People & Culture needs additional information to prepare the document, we will follow up.

Employees who worked in Georgia, South Carolina, North Carolina, or Virginia may submit a ticket. People & Culture will review the request and follow up if more information is needed.

How does Holiday Pay appear on my pay stub?

Holiday Pay may appear separately from regular hours and may not look like additional hours on the pay statement. Eligibility and calculation rules may change by benefit year.

For Thanksgiving 2026, eligible Shift Leaders and Crew Members with over one year of continuous service receive 1.5 times their normal hourly rate for hours worked on November 26, 2026. The amount appears as Holiday Pay on the pay statement. This benefit applies only when no overtime is scheduled or worked during the November 23–29, 2026 payroll week. These are the current 2026 Thanksgiving rules; do not assume they apply to another holiday or benefit year.

If you believe Holiday Pay is missing, submit a Support Ticket for Holiday Pay. Include the holiday worked, hours worked, pay date, and what appears on your pay statement.

Where can I see information about the benefits offered to employees?

Start with the current Employee Benefits Guide for your position/classification. It explains the benefit programs available for the current plan year.

The updated Employee Benefits Guide will be added here when available.

For a question about personal eligibility, enrollment, deductions, or existing coverage, submit a Benefits Support Ticket.
