People & Culture

Payday. Paperwork. The people part of working here.

Start with a quick answer.

These are a few of the questions we hear most. Find yours below, or reach out if you need us to take a closer look.

Where do I find my pay stubs?

Open Workstream Worker Hub and sign in to view your pay information. Use Worker Hub for employee records; Workstream Hire is the hiring system.

Something on my paycheck doesn’t look right. Where do I start?

If the issue is missing time or a shift, start with your General Manager so they can review the restaurant’s time records.

If you still need help, contact People & Culture with your name, restaurant location, the pay date, and what looks incorrect. Those details help us review the right payment.

Where do I find my tax documents?

Start in Workstream Worker Hub. If the document you need isn’t available, contact People & Culture and tell us which tax year you need help with.

I can’t sign in. Who can help?

For Worker Hub, start with the Workstream help guides. If you still need assistance, tell People & Culture which system you’re trying to open and the exact error you see.

For the manager SharePoint site, use your individual company account. The public Georgia Foods Bo-nnection homepage does not require a Microsoft sign-in.

Who handles my schedule or a time-off request?

Start with your General Manager for schedule questions and time-off planning. You can open Altametrics from Georgia Foods Bo-nnection.

If you need help understanding your vacation balance or a leave question, contact People & Culture. Check with your manager before making plans that depend on time-off approval.

I have a benefits question. What should I include?

Send your name, restaurant location, and the benefit you have a question about. Tell us whether you need help with enrollment, an existing benefit, or finding the right contact.

Can I contact People & Culture about a workplace concern?

Yes. You can text 800-484-1622 or email help@gaf-boj.com. Include your name, restaurant location, and what you would like us to review.

When should I expect a response?

People & Culture is available weekdays from 9 AM to 4 PM Eastern. Allow up to one business day for a response.

One text or email with the details is enough to get started. If you already reached out, continue in that same conversation so your information stays together.
